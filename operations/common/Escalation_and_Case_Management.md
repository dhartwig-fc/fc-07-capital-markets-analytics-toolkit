# Escalation and Case Management Framework

## Purpose

This document defines the operational lifecycle for Capital Markets surveillance alerts and investigations.

---

## Alert Lifecycle

```text
Alert Generated
        ↓
Level 1 Review
        ↓
Level 2 Investigation
        ↓
SME Assessment
        ↓
Decision
        ↓
Case Closure / STOR
```

---

## Level 1 Review

### Objectives

- Validate alert quality
- Confirm data availability
- Identify obvious false positives

### Outcomes

- Close Alert
- Escalate to Level 2

---

## Level 2 Investigation

### Objectives

- Conduct detailed order-flow and trade review
- Assess trader/account behaviour
- Review counterparties and beneficial ownership
- Analyse supporting documentation (order book, communications, market data)

### Outputs

- Investigation Summary
- Risk Assessment
- Recommendation

---

## SME Review

Required when:

- Complex cross-venue or cross-instrument structures exist
- Sanctions or PEP exposure identified
- Significant order/trade values involved
- Regulatory concerns identified

Reviewers may include:

- Trade Surveillance SME
- Market Abuse SME
- Sanctions Specialist

---

## Escalation Triggers

### High Priority

- Sanctions or PEP indicators
- Coordinated manipulation network indicators
- Cross-venue / cross-instrument layering indicators

### Medium Priority

- Significant volume/velocity anomalies
- Third-party order activity
- Unusual account behaviour changes

### Low Priority

- Minor documentation inconsistencies

---

## STOR Escalation

Cases should be referred for Suspicious Transaction and Order Report (STOR) assessment where:

- Suspicion threshold met
- Market abuse suspected
- Concealment behaviour identified

---

## Case Closure

Case closure should include:

- Investigation summary
- Findings
- Risk assessment
- Final decision
- Approvals

---

## Management Information

Recommended MI metrics:

- Alerts generated
- Alerts closed
- Escalation rate
- STOR referral rate
- Average investigation time
- False positive rate
